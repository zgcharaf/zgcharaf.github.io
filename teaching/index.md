# Teaching

I enjoy building inclusive, activity-driven learning experiences that help students connect theoretical ideas with real-world scenarios. Below are a few sample resources that illustrate the structure of my workshops and courses.

## Course Philosophy

- **Active learning first.** Every session includes group problem-solving and reflective prompts.
- **Practical connections.** Case studies and datasets are chosen to mirror the challenges students will see in internships and research labs.
- **Iterative feedback.** Short feedback loops (polls, muddiest point summaries) steer the pacing of each module.

## How I Use These Resources

1. **Pre-class preparation:** Students skim the lecture outline to preview new terminology.
2. **In-class engagement:** We iterate between mini-lectures and the interactive activity so teams can test their understanding.
3. **Post-class reflection:** Students adapt the slides with their own solutions and submit a short summary of their takeaways.
