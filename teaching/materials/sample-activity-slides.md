# Sample Activity Slides

This markdown file outlines the flow of an interactive problem-solving activity that can be converted into slides.

## Slide 1 — Challenge Brief
- Title: "Design a Fair Matching Algorithm"
- Prompt students to read the problem and list unknowns.

## Slide 2 — Team Roles
- Facilitator keeps time and guides discussion.
- Scribe records the team's proposed algorithm steps.
- Reporter prepares to summarize the solution.

## Slide 3 — Iteration Timer
- Display a five-minute countdown.
- Include reminder: "Pause halfway to check assumptions."

## Slide 4 — Hint Bank
- Provide two optional hints that can be revealed after three minutes.
- Encourage teams to document why they used a hint.

## Slide 5 — Debrief Questions
1. What invariant did your team rely on?
2. Where could bias creep into the matching process?
3. How would you test your algorithm with real data?

## Slide 6 — Reflection Prompt
- Students complete a short reflection in their notes.
- Suggested question: "What did you learn from another team today?"

Use this structure as a starting point, then export to your preferred slide format.
