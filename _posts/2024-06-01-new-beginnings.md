Starting with a blank notebook is always tempting, but the real progress comes from keeping the
pieces that still serve the work. This new version of the site keeps the essentials—clear typography,
math support, and a straightforward archive—so I can focus on writing about economics and data.

To make sure the workflow is ready for problem sets and derivations, every post now understands
LaTeX. For example, the variance of the sample mean \(\bar{x}\) under independent draws \(x_i\) with
variance \(\sigma^2\) is rendered automatically:

$$
\operatorname{Var}(\bar{x}) = \operatorname{Var}\left(\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^n x_i\right) = \frac{\sigma^2}{n}.
$$

I am rebuilding the archive with concise technical notes, reproducible code, and references that I
can share with collaborators. If you have ideas worth exploring together, feel free to reach out.
