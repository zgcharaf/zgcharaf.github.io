This is the second time I attend Pydata Paris, some aspects of the conference changed a bit while others did thankfully remain the same. The energy of the attendies, the openesss, the welcoming nature of the community for folks from both academia and industry and the interesting thought provoking talks are one of the aspects that did not change.

There's something energizing about clearing the slate and rebuilding from first principles. Over the
past few years my personal site evolved into a loose collection of lecture notes and unfinished
ideas. It no longer reflected the work I want to share or the way I collaborate.

This relaunch marks a fresh start. I'm focusing on:

- Writing concise essays about the questions I'm exploring.
- Publishing polished research notes and resources I rely on.
- Documenting small projects that help me learn faster.

If you'd like to follow along, subscribe to the RSS feed or reach out—I'd love to connect.
