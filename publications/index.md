# Publications & Talks

A selection of recent writing, talks, and collaborations. For a complete list or supporting materials, please reach out directly.

## Journal Articles

- **Adaptive Liquidity Provision in Frontier Markets** (2023). *Journal of Quantitative Finance Practice*. Co-authored with Lina Haddad. Explores order book resiliency and inventory management across thinly traded equities.
- **Stress Testing Systemic Risk with Probabilistic Graphs** (2021). *Risk Analytics Review*. Demonstrates how Bayesian networks can uncover contagion channels between financial institutions.

## Working Papers

- **Measuring Sentiment Spillovers from Policy Announcements** (in review). Investigates how central bank communications impact cross-asset volatility regimes using text embeddings.
- **Robust Portfolio Construction with Sparse Factors** (in preparation). Develops a convex optimization approach for integrating macro signals into factor models.
