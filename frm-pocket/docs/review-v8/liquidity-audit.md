## Review method and limits

The supplied extraction has 425 PDF pages. PDF page numbers in JSON are physical 1-based PDF pages; printed content pages are generally physical page minus 15. The table of contents and every chapter's objective page were checked against the current readings. Substantive chapter text, equations, cases, summaries and available end problems were reviewed using page-indexed passage retrieval across the ranges below. Priority was given to mechanisms, mathematical definitions, assumptions, decision tradeoffs and plausible distractors rather than long historical narratives.

This was a section-oriented review of extracted text with targeted PDF-image confirmation, not a word-for-word proofread of every paragraph or every solved textbook exercise. Multi-column text extraction can reorder statements, lose fraction structure and misread symbols. Figures, historical statistics, obsolete website exercises and every table cell have not all been independently reconstructed. Bibliographies/index pages were identified but not studied as learning content. The audit makes no claim of GARP exam equivalence, empirical item-difficulty calibration or complete coverage of every possible problem type.

A direct rendered image of physical PDF page 276 was inspected because its AFG arithmetic looked inconsistent. Formula generation in the new material uses independent derivations and recomputations rather than trusting an OCR expression. No PDF pages or textbook text are intended for publication in the app.

## Current-syllabus differences

All 17 2026 LTR readings have counterparts in this 2024 book. This does not establish identical text editions, only matching readings and objective concepts from the supplied official scope. The objective wording for LTR-5 no longer includes the two old reserve-calculation/source objectives found on 2024 PDF page 102. The dedicated 2024 deposit-services chapter is not a current standalone LTR reading. Its removal shifts 2024 book chapters 13–18 to LTR-12–17. Illiquid Assets maps to Investment IM-17.

Current-paper/2026-only topics from other books are outside this bounded liquidity task. None of the financial-crisis numerical statistics, US reserve schedules, FDIC assessments, LIBOR examples or historical fails rules is asserted to be a present-day rule. Cases either use stable mechanisms or define all rates, haircuts, taxes, quotation conventions and eligibility assumptions explicitly.

## Source inconsistency and formula cautions

1. **PDF page 276 / printed 261:** the typeset AFG example lists uses 150+75+135 and resources 185+100, then prints “306−285=75.” The uses sum is 360, not 306; 360−285=75 is the correct final calculation. The discrepancy was verified on a rendered PNG, not blamed automatically on OCR. New xl046 uses independently authored amounts and guards against double-counted commitment draws.
2. **Spread risk, page 55:** use the volatility of the full proportional spread under the explicitly supplied approximation, then divide the entire stressed spread by two. Percentage points must be converted before multiplication by market value.
3. **Quantile aggregation, pages 165–169:** no assertion that sums of separate VaRs are exact or guaranteed upper bounds. The new question explicitly requires joint dependence; VaR need not be subadditive.
4. **Repo, pages 289–300:** price plus accrued interest gives dirty value; haircut determines cash; interest is on cash on the stated day count. Financing premium uses cash after haircut and DV01 in currency per bp.
5. **CIP, pages 350–351:** quotation is explicit USD per EUR and both spot/forward units are carried through the replication. A synthetic funding premium is not labeled with an unspecified market basis-sign convention.
6. **ALM, pages 381–389:** modified durations do not receive a second division by one plus yield. EVE combines asset and liability dollar durations, while NII cases include repricing timing and pass-through separately.

The seven dimension-balancing questions xl079–xl085 were added subsequently, passed the same schema/math checks and received an independent stem/option/key review with no further correction required.
